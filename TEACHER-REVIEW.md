Status: **Draft teaching material — requires review by a qualified English teacher before
use with real learners.** This file lists every item the final production audit

## How to read this list

- **Context flips are intentional.** The same word can be a correct answer in one
  question and a distractor in another (e.g. "fish" is correct for *Slice the
  animals* but a distractor for *Slice the fruits*). This teaches that correctness
  depends on the question — keep it.
- The items below are judgement calls about vocabulary framing and classroom fit,
  not errors. Review and either confirm or propose replacement wording.

## Items to review

### 1. `l01-q12` — "Slice the animals that can fly." (Level 1)
Correct: bird, bee, butterfly. Bees and butterflies are insects, which are animals
scientifically, but younger learners may not classify insects as "animals".
Decide whether the classroom framing suits the target age group.

### 2. `l01-q13` — "Slice the pets." (Level 1)
Correct: cat, dog, fish. Fish as a pet is standard in most classrooms, but confirm
it matches the course's vocabulary framing.

### 3. `l05-q02` — "Slice the action verbs." (Level 5)
Distractor "water"; explanation says "water is a noun here". Water can also be a
verb (*water the plants*). The explanation already scopes it with "here" — confirm
this level of nuance is right for A2, or swap the distractor for an unambiguous noun.

### 4. Repeated prompt "Slice the furniture."
Used twice inside Level 1 (`l01-q06`, `l01-q07` — different option sets) and once in
Level 4 (`l04-q13`). Repetition with variation is sound for young learners and the
cross-level repeat acts as spaced practice; confirm this is intended.

### 5. Dual part-of-speech distractors (systematic pattern)
Many distractors are words that can be verbs or nouns (watch, book, play, water,
work, shop, cook, dream, train). Every explanation clarifies the intended reading.
This is desirable for grammar awareness but relies on the player reading the
explanation. Spot-check a sample, especially:
- `l01-q08` (watch as a thing you use), `l03-q13` (book carried by students),
- `l07-q02` (work as a daily action), `l09-q09` (train as a workplace verb).

### 6. CEFR labels
Stage labels A1/A2/B1 are design targets, not certified CEFR alignments. A teacher
should confirm the level placements match the course's syllabus.
