How to play

  1. Read the challenge on the hanging banner.
  2. Swipe through fruits whose word matches — tap or drag, both work.
  3. Leave other fruits untouched; wrong slices cost a heart.
  4. Keep the streak — 3+ correct in a row doubles your points.
  5. Pass levels to unlock the next one and earn stars.
